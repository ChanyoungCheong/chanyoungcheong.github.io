About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate investigating the neural mechanisms underlying brain states and behavior.

My research focuses on sleep-wake regulation, neuropathic pain, and neuromodulation techniques using mouse models. I use systems neuroscience approaches, including electrophysiology, optogenetics, and fiber photometry, to investigate the neural circuits and neuromodulatory systems underlying these processes.
